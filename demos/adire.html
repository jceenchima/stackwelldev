The fabric room

OUR DYE HOUSE

Every yard passes through twelve hands.

We work with 40 women-led dye cooperatives in Ogun State. Buying from Adire & Co. pays artisans fairly and keeps a centuries-old Yoruba craft alive.

1

Design & resist

Patterns are drawn with cassava paste or tied with raffia.

2

Indigo bath

Cloth is dipped up to 15 times in fermented indigo vats.

3

Wash & finish

Paste is scraped away and the fabric is sun-dried and pressed.
