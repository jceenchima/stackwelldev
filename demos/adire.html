Inside the dye house

Four days from white cotton to adire.

1

Draw the design

The artist marks out the grid and motifs on plain white cotton with charcoal.

2

Paint the resist

Cassava starch paste is brushed on with a feather or a stencil. Wherever the paste sits, indigo cannot reach.

3

Dip in the vat

The cloth goes into fermented indigo again and again, drying in the sun between dips. Each dip darkens the blue.

4

Wash and reveal

The paste is scraped off and the cloth washed. The pale pattern appears against the deep blue.
